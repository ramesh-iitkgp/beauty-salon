Do I need a consultation before my appointment?

We provide a complimentary 1-on-1 consultation at the start of every appointment. For dramatic color changes, color corrections, or extensions, we recommend booking a dedicated 15-minute consultation beforehand.

What professional products and hair color do you use?

We exclusively use cruelty-free, luxury organic professional formulas including Olaplex bond repair, Davines botanicals, and certified clinical skincare serums.
