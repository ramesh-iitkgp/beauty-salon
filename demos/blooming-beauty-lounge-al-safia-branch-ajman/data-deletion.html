User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform policies and global data privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA), users have the right to request the deletion of any personal data collected through WebKartX integrations, including WhatsApp Business API communications.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Automatic Opt-Out: Send a WhatsApp message with the keyword STOP to our business number. This revokes messaging consent and suppresses your contact number from any future automated notifications.
  2. Complete Purge Request: Send an email to ramesh25@kgpian.iitkgp.ac.in with the subject line Data Deletion Request, including the phone number or email address you wish to have removed.
  3. Confirmation: Our technical team will permanently purge your message logs, conversation records, and contact information from our databases within 48 business hours and send you a confirmation receipt.
